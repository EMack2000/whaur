How to play

Five Scottish places a day. Tap the map where you think each one is, then lock it in. The closer your pin, the more of the round's 100 points you keep.

The places get harder as you go, and the later rounds count for more: rounds one and two count once, round three counts double, rounds four and five count triple. That makes a total out of 1,000.

Your pin is out byPoints

Pinch, scroll or use the buttons to zoom, and drag to move the map. The zoom stops before you can make out roads. England and Ireland are greyed out, and Shetland is where it really is, not in a box.
